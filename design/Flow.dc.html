The nav does not say what the app does

Eight destinations, all peers, in an order nobody chose. Four of them are different ways to find work; three are stages of one pipeline. Nothing says which is which, or where a release currently is.

“Explore” and “Scan” do not say what they explore or scan. “Found” is a noun with no verb. A run waiting on an answer is invisible from seven of these eight.
PROPOSED — TWO GROUPS, ONE OF THEM IN ORDER
FIND WORK — ENTER ANYWHERE
Search — one release by name
Browse — charts and channels
Scan — a list against a tracker
Requests — what people asked for
Renamed for what they do, not what they are. All four end the same way: they add to the queue.

2  Downloading 2
pulling from Deezer

1 needs an answer
Numbered because the order is real — nothing reaches 3 without passing 2. The counts are always visible, so the rail answers “is anything waiting on me?” from every screen in the app.
THE ONE THING THAT ALWAYS INTERRUPTS
An upload stops and asks you something — lossy master, which group, fill a request. NEEDS YOU
Today this is only visible if you are already looking at Uploads. It becomes a count on the rail and a badge in the tab title, so a run cannot sit blocked for an hour without you knowing.
